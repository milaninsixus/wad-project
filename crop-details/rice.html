Rice / Paddy Cultivation Guide (ડાંગર)

Agronomic package for high-yield paddy production in Central and South Gujarat: Nursery raising, puddle transplanting, SRI methods, zinc nutrition, and stem borer IPM.

1. Overview & Agricultural Context

Paddy (Oryza sativa), known as Dangar (ડાંગર), is the staple food grain crop grown primarily in the canal-command areas and fertile plains of Central and South Gujarat, encompassing Kheda, Anand, Ahmedabad, Navsari, Valsad, and Surat. High production relies on quality nursery raising, puddle puddling, balanced nitrogen timing, and strict water conservation.

2. Climate, Soil & Varieties

Soil Requirements: Heavy clayey or clay-loam alluvial soils with poor water infiltration (subsurface hardpan) are ideal to retain submerged water conditions without excessive percolation losses. Soil pH range of 5.5 to 7.5 is optimal.

  • Prominent Gujarat Varieties: Gurjari, GR-11, GR-12, GR-7, Jaya, Dandi (Saline coastal soils), and GAR-13.

3. Nursery Raising & Transplanting

Method Nursery Area Needed Seed Rate Transplanting Age
Conventional Transplanting 1,000 m² (0.1 ha) / ha field 30–35 kg/ha 21 to 25 days (3–4 leaf stage)
SRI (System of Rice Intensification) 100 m² / ha field 5–7 kg/ha 8 to 12 days (2-leaf single seedling)

Field Spacing: Transplant 2–3 seedlings per hill at 20 cm x 15 cm spacing. For SRI, plant 1 seedling per hill in a square pattern at 25 cm x 25 cm to maximize tillering.

4. Nutrient Management & Mandatory Zinc Application

Recommended NPK Dose: 100–120 kg N : 30–40 kg P₂O₅ : 0–30 kg K₂O per hectare.

  • Apply all Phosphorus and Potash basally during final puddling.
  • Nitrogen Splits: Apply Nitrogen in 3 splits: 50% at final puddling, 25% at active tillering (21 DAT), and 25% at panicle initiation (45–50 DAT).
  • Zinc Deficiency (Khaira Disease): Highly prevalent in puddled calcareous soils. Apply 25 kg/ha Zinc Sulfate (ZnSO₄) basally at final puddling (never mix directly with DAP or SSP).

5. Water Management: Alternating Wetting & Drying (AWD)

Maintain shallow water (2–3 cm) during first week of transplanting for establishment. Thereafter, practice Alternate Wetting & Drying (AWD)—re-irrigating to 5 cm depth only when water has naturally receded to 15 cm below soil surface. Maintain continuous 3–5 cm water strictly during flowering. Drain field completely 10–12 days prior to harvest.

6. Pest & Disease Management

  • Yellow Stem Borer: Larvae bore into stem producing "dead hearts" in vegetative stage and empty "white heads" at panicle stage. Install 8 pheromone traps/ha. Apply Chlorantraniliprole 0.4% G (10 kg/ha) or Cartap Hydrochloride 4% G at 30 DAT.
  • Blast Disease (Pyricularia oryzae): Spindle-shaped lesions with ash-colored centers. Spray Tricyclazole 75 WP (0.6 g/L) or Azoxystrobin (1 ml/L).
  • Brown Plant Hopper (BPH): Avoid dense planting and excessive urea. Maintain 30 cm alleyways every 2–3 meters. Spray Pymetrozine 50 WG (0.6 g/L) directing spray at base of hills.

7. Harvesting & Post-Harvest Storage

Harvest when 80–85% grains in panicles turn golden yellow. Dry threshed paddy uniformly to 14% moisture for safe storage or milling to prevent grain cracking.
