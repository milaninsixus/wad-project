Government Schemes & Farmer Services Directory

Official repository of Central and Gujarat State agricultural welfare schemes, direct benefit transfer (DBT) programs, subsidized equipment initiatives, and toll-free helplines.

Official Portal Policy Notice: KrushiMitra connects farmers directly to verified official government domains (.gov.in / .nic.in). We never ask for Aadhaar OTPs, banking PINs, or processing fees. Program eligibility, payment approval, and subsidy disbursement are subject entirely to physical document verification by relevant district authorities.
Central Government DBT Verified: Oct 2026

PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

Direct income support transferring ₹6,000 per year into the bank accounts of all landholding farmer families across the country in 3 equal four-monthly installments of ₹2,000 each.

Eligibility & Documents: All landholding farmer families with cultivable land in revenue records. Institutional landholders and high-income taxpayers excluded. Mandatory: Aadhaar card, land 7/12 record, and active bank account with eKYC completion.
Visit pmkisan.gov.in ↗ Helpline: 155261
Crop Insurance Verified: Oct 2026

PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana)

Comprehensive crop insurance protecting farmers against yield losses due to non-preventable natural risks (drought, dry spells, flood, pest outbreaks, unseasonal rains, and post-harvest cyclone damage).

Premium Rates: Maximum 2.0% of sum insured for Kharif food and oilseed crops, 1.5% for Rabi crops, and 5% for annual commercial/horticultural crops. Balance premium subsidized equally by Central and State Governments.
Visit pmfby.gov.in ↗ Claim within 72 hrs
Central Scheme • Soil Health Verified: Oct 2026

Soil Health Card Scheme

Provides farmers with printed Soil Health Cards detailing the nutrient status of their individual farm holdings across 12 crucial parameters, along with customized crop-wise fertilizer dosage recommendations.

12 Parameters Tested: Macro-nutrients (N, P, K), secondary nutrients (S), micronutrients (Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B), and physical parameters (pH, Electrical Conductivity, Organic Carbon).
Soil Health Portal ↗ Issued Every 3 Years
Digital Mandi Trading Verified: Oct 2026

e-NAM (National Agriculture Market)

Pan-India electronic trading portal networking physical APMC mandis into a unified national market. Enables transparent online bidding, digital assaying, and direct online payment to farmer accounts.

Key Benefit: Real-time discovery of competitive commodity prices across mandis in Gujarat (Gondal, Rajkot, Unjha, Surat) and other states, eliminating local buyer cartels.
Visit enam.gov.in ↗ Over 1,300 Mandis
Gujarat State Portal Verified: Oct 2026

Gujarat i-Khedut Portal (આઈ-ખેડૂત પોર્ટલ)

Unified state web portal by the Department of Agriculture, Government of Gujarat. Farmers submit single-window online applications for state farm equipment subsidies, tractor grants, water storage tanks, and solar pumps.

Available Subsidies: Tractor purchase (up to ₹45,000–₹60,000 subsidy), rotavators, power tillers, certified hybrid seeds, cow rearing sahay (₹900/month for indigenous desi cow), and pipeline grants.
ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in ↗ 7/12 & 8-A Required
Nationwide Advisory Service Verified: Oct 2026

Kisan Call Centre (KCC Helpline)

Toll-free telephonic agricultural extension service operated by the Ministry of Agriculture. Farmers speak directly to agricultural graduates (Level-1) and subject matter specialists (Level-2) in their regional language.

Operating Hours & Languages: Dial 1800-180-1551 from any mobile or landline network. Active 7 days a week from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM in Gujarati, Hindi, and 20 other national languages.
