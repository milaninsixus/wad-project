Agricultural & Legal Disclaimer

Statutory terms governing usage of agricultural recommendations, chemical pesticide safety information, and government scheme listings on KrushiMitra.

It is not an individual professional agronomic consultation or an on-site crop diagnosis.

2. Pesticide & Agrochemical Safety Mandate

Pesticides, insecticides, fungicides, and herbicides are hazardous chemical substances governed by the Insecticides Act, 1968. Users must strictly read and adhere to the physical container label and leaflet approved by the Central Insecticide Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) before opening, handling, or applying any chemical product. KrushiMitra explicitly disclaims liability for crop phytotoxicity, personal injury, environmental contamination, or livestock poisoning arising from incorrect dosage, unauthorized tank mixtures, or off-label use.

3. Climatic, Soil & Yield Variations

Agricultural production outcomes depend heavily on uncontrollable environmental parameters including microclimate variations, unseasonal rainfall, drought, pest resistance mutations, and soil salinity. Recommendations published on this website cannot guarantee specific quintal yields or financial returns. Always consult your Gram Sevak, local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK), or District Agriculture Officer for farm-specific adjustments.

4. Government Schemes & Subsidies

Scheme details, eligibility criteria, and financial assistance amounts are summarized from official central and state government notifications. KrushiMitra does not guarantee scheme approval, installment release, or loan sanction. All administrative authority rests exclusively with the relevant government departments.
