Agricultural Medicines & Chemical Safety Directory

Official registered compound profiles compliant with Central Insecticide Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC) standards. Strict safety equipment, pre-harvest intervals, and environmental guidelines.

STATUTORY AGROCHEMICAL USE & STEWARDSHIP NOTICE

1. Follow Exact Label: Dosage, water volume, and registered crops must strictly follow the official container label approved by the CIBRC. Different commercial formulations of the same active ingredient carry different concentrations.
2. No Unverified Tank Mixtures: Never mix two chemical pesticides without authorized compatibility verification on the product leaflet.
3. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE): Always wear a respirator mask, protective chemical goggles, heavy nitrile rubber gloves, and rubber gumboots when measuring and spraying.
4. Safe Storage: Keep all agro-chemicals securely locked away from children, food, animal feed, and potable drinking water wells.
5. Professional Advisory: KrushiMitra is an educational catalog and does NOT substitute for qualified Agricultural Officers or Plant Protection Officers.

Bio-Pesticide • Botanical Extract

Neem Oil 10,000 PPM (Azadirachtin 1% EC)

CIBRC Registered Botanical Formulations
✓ Eco-Friendly / Pollinator Safe
Target Pests: Aphids, Whiteflies, Jassids, Early Caterpillars
Registered Crops: Cotton, Cumin, Mustard, Groundnut, Vegetables
Mode of Action: Ecdysone blocker, anti-feedant, oviposition deterrent
Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 0 to 3 Days
Re-Entry Interval (REI): 4 Hours
Spray Volume: 500 L water/ha (dilution: 2.0 to 3.0 ml/L water)
Label-Approved Guidance: Thoroughly agitate the formulation before mixing. Spray in the late afternoon to prevent rapid UV degradation of active azadirachtin isomers. Safe for predatory ladybird beetles, green lacewings, and pollinating honeybees.
First-Aid: In case of eye contact, rinse with clean water for 15 minutes. Non-hazardous when used according to label instructions. CIBRC Schedule ↗
Organophosphate Insecticide

Chlorpyrifos 20% EC

Caution Class: Moderately Hazardous (Blue Triangle)
⚠ Restricted Soil/Termite Use
Target Pests: Termites, White Grubs, Cutworms
Registered Crops: Wheat seed treatment, Groundnut soil drench, Sugarcane
Mode of Action: Acetylcholinesterase (AChE) inhibitor
Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 30 Days (Soil application)
Re-Entry Interval (REI): 24 Hours
Dosage (Label): 3 to 4 L/ha in soil application, or 4 ml/kg seed for termites
Environmental & Safety Precautions: Highly toxic to aquatic ecosystems and fish. Never wash spray equipment or empty drums near ponds, borewells, or open drainage canals. Keep out of reach of domestic animals and grazing livestock.
First-Aid & Antidote: If swallowed, induce vomiting if patient is conscious. Specific medical antidote: Atropine Sulfate administered intravenously under physician care. CIBRC Portal ↗
Systemic Neonicotinoid Insecticide

Imidacloprid 17.8% SL

Acropetal Systemic Protection
⚠ Toxic to Honeybees
Target Pests: Aphids, Jassids, Thrips, Brown Plant Hopper
Registered Crops: Cotton, Groundnut, Paddy, Chilli, Cumin
Mode of Action: Nicotinic acetylcholine receptor (nAChR) agonist
Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 21–40 Days (Crop specific)
Re-Entry Interval (REI): 12 Hours
Dosage (Label): 100 to 125 ml/ha (dilution: 0.3 to 0.5 ml/L water)
Pollinator Protection Warning: Highly toxic to foraging honeybees. Strictly avoid spraying during morning hours when crop flowers are in active bloom. Wait until late evening after bees return to hives.
First-Aid: Wash skin contaminated with spray immediately with copious soap and clean water. Antidote is symptomatic. CIBRC Specification ↗
Broad-Spectrum Contact Fungicide

Mancozeb 75% WP

Dithiocarbamate Multi-Site Inhibitor
✓ Low Resistance Risk
Target Diseases: Tikka Leaf Spot, Early & Late Blight, Downy Mildew
Registered Crops: Groundnut, Cumin, Wheat, Potato, Tomato, Cotton
Mode of Action: Inactivates essential fungal enzymes and amino acids (Multi-site)
Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 15–20 Days
Re-Entry Interval (REI): 24 Hours
Dosage (Label): 1.5 to 2.0 kg/ha (dilution: 2.0 to 2.5 g/L water)
Application Note: Functions as a protective shield. Must be sprayed *prior* to fungal spore entry. Uniform coverage on both upper and lower leaf surfaces is vital.
PPE: Use rubber gloves, dust respirator, and face mask while preparing suspension from wettable powder. CIBRC Registry ↗
Dual-Action Systemic Fungicide

Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC

Strobilurin (QoI) + Triazole (DMI) Systemic Combination
✓ Curative & Preventive
Target Diseases: Powdery Mildew, Alternaria Blight, Rust, Blast
Registered Crops: Cumin, Wheat, Paddy, Chilli, Tomato
Mode of Action: Inhibits mitochondrial respiration + ergosterol biosynthesis
Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI): 21 Days
Re-Entry Interval (REI): 24 Hours
Dosage (Label): 500 ml/ha (dilution: 1.0 ml/L water)
Resistance Management (FRAC Guidelines): To prevent resistant fungal strain development, do not apply this combination more than twice per crop season. Alternate with contact fungicides like Mancozeb or Sulfur.
Precautions: Wear eye protection and waterproof boots. Avoid inhaling spray mist. CIBRC Schedule ↗
Pre-Emergence Selective Herbicide

Pendimethalin 30% EC

Dinitroaniline Microtubule Assembly Inhibitor
⚠ Soil Moisture Essential
Target Weeds: Annual grasses (Echinochloa, Digitaria) & broadleaf weeds
Registered Crops: Cotton, Groundnut, Wheat, Cumin, Soybean, Onion
Mode of Action: Inhibits root and shoot mitosis in emerging weed seeds
Application Timing: Strictly within 24–48 hours of sowing, before crop/weed emergence
Re-Entry Interval (REI): 24 Hours
Dosage (Label): 2.5 to 3.3 L/ha (diluted in 500 L water/ha using flat-fan nozzle)
Application Prerequisite: Soil must have good moisture at the time of spray for chemical activation. Use a flat-fan or flood-jet nozzle with a protective spray hood to ensure a continuous herbicide barrier over the soil surface. Do not disturb the soil layer after application.
PPE: Wear chemical-resistant apron, nitrile gloves, and face shield during mixing and nozzle calibration. CIBRC Registry ↗
