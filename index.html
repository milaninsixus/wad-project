Current Season: Rabi Crop Planning & Winter Sowing

Recommended for Gujarat, Rajasthan, and Western MP belts.

Rabi Focus • Active Planning
🌱

Certified Seed Selection & Fungicide Coating

Always treat wheat seed with Carboxin + Thiram (2g/kg) and cumin seed with Trichoderma viride (10g/kg) against seed-borne wilt and flag smut.

💧

Crown Root Initiation (CRI) Irrigation

First irrigation in wheat must strictly occur 20-25 days after sowing at CRI stage. Missing this irrigation permanently restricts tillering capacity.

⚠️

Cumin Powdery Mildew Vigilance

Inspect umbels during December cool spells. Prophylactic dusting of 300-mesh sulfur powder (25 kg/ha) prevents catastrophic crop loss.

Sample Educational Advisory: Sowing dates depend strictly on soil temperature and onset of winter chill. Always confirm local variety selections with your District Agriculture Officer or Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK).

Monsoon Kharif Operations & Drainage

Cotton, Groundnut, Paddy, and Castor management.

Kharif Guide
🚜

Deep Summer Ploughing

Expose soil to intense May-June heat to destroy hibernating pupae of cotton bollworm and soil-borne fungal spores naturally.

🌧️

Sowing After 75-100mm Rainfall

Avoid pre-monsoon dry sowing in non-irrigated black soils. Wait until soil moisture penetrates at least 15-20 cm deep.

🪱

White Grub Soil Treatment

In groundnut endemic tracts, treat seed with Imidacloprid 600 FS or incorporate Beauveria bassiana bio-agent in farmyard manure.

Summer (Zaid) Short-Duration Crops

Summer Bajra, Moong (Green Gram), and Summer Groundnut.

Summer Guide
☀️

Irrigation In Peak Evaporation

Schedule micro-irrigation in early mornings or evenings to curb water evaporation losses by over 30% during hot summer winds.

🌿

Moong as Soil Enhancer

Cultivate 60-day summer green gram (moong) after wheat harvest. Fixes up to 40 kg biological nitrogen per hectare for the upcoming kharif crop.

🌾

Summer Pearl Millet (Bajra)

High grain recovery with reduced ergot infection due to dry air. Ensure moisture during boot and grain-filling stages.

Pest • High Impact

Pink & American Bollworm

Affected: Cotton, Okra Type: Larval Borer

Larvae enter young squares and bolls, plugging entry holes with frass. Causes rosette flowers, boll rotting, and stained unmarketable lint.

Use Pheromone Traps (5/ha) Inspect Symptoms →
Sucking Pest

Aphids (મોલો-મશી / Molo)

Affected: Cumin, Mustard, Cotton Type: Homopteran

Colonies cluster under leaves and on young inflorescences, sucking vital sap. Secretes sticky honeydew attracting black sooty mold fungus.

Early Spray: Neem Oil 10,000 PPM Inspect Symptoms →
Fungal Disease

Powdery Mildew (છારો રોગ)

Affected: Cumin, Coriander, Vegetables Type: Erysiphales

White talcum powder-like mycelial patches emerge on leaves, stems, and flower umbels. Leads to seed shriveling and near-total crop loss in cumin.

Preventive: Wettable Sulfur 80% WP Inspect Symptoms →

CRITICAL PESTICIDE SAFETY & LEGAL COMPLIANCE NOTICE

Never spray without complete Personal Protective Equipment (gloves, eye goggles, N95 respiratory mask, full boots). Always follow the registered container label approved by the CIBRC. Keep all agro-chemicals securely locked away from children, livestock, and open water bodies. Dosage depends strictly on crop stage, spray volume, and variety.

Bio-Pesticide • Organic Approved

Neem Oil 10,000 PPM (Azadirachtin 1%)

Botanical Anti-feedant & Oviposition Deterrent

Ideal for sucking pest suppression (aphids, whiteflies, thrips) and early caterpillar instars. Harmless to predatory honeybees and beneficial spiders.

PHI: 0-3 Days • Crops: Cotton, Cumin, Vegetables

Neonicotinoid Systemic Compound

Label registered for severe sucking pest outbreaks in cotton and paddy. Translocates acropetally through plant xylem tissues.

Caution: Highly toxic to pollinating honeybees. Avoid spraying during active flowering periods.
