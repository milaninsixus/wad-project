Crop Cultivation Directory

Science-backed agronomic guides for major cash crops, oilseeds, cereals, and spices cultivated in Gujarat and semi-arid agro-ecological zones of Western India.

Kharif • Cash Crop

Cotton (કપાસ / Gossypium)

⏱️ 150-180 Days 🌱 Deep Black Cotton Soil 📏 120 x 45 cm Spacing

Premier cash crop of Gujarat (Saurashtra and Central belts). High yield demands balanced NPK (120:60:60 kg/ha), drip irrigation, and timely bollworm interception.

Kharif / Summer • Oilseed

Groundnut (મગફળી / Arachis hypogaea)

⏱️ 105-120 Days 🌱 Well-Drained Sandy Loam 📏 30-45 x 10 cm Spacing

The lifeblood of Saurashtra's farming economy. Critical phases are flowering, peg penetration, and pod filling. Requires 250 kg/ha Gypsum at 30-35 DAS.

Semi-spreading & Bunch types Complete Guide →
Rabi • Cereal Staple

Wheat (ઘઉં / Triticum aestivum)

⏱️ 110-125 Days 🌱 Clay Loam to Alluvial 📏 20-22.5 cm Row Spacing

Winter cereal mainstay across Bhal, Mehsana, and Sabarkantha. 5-6 irrigations aligned with crown root initiation, tillering, flowering, and milk stages.

GW-496, GW-322, Bhal Daudkhani Complete Guide →
Kharif • Cereal Food Grain

Rice / Paddy (ડાંગર / Oryza sativa)

⏱️ 120-140 Days 🌱 Clayey Alluvial Soil 📏 20 x 15 cm Spacing

High-water staple across Kheda, Anand, and Navsari districts. Requires puddle transplanting, 25 kg/ha Zinc Sulfate, and careful stem borer management.

Gurjari, GR-11, Dandi Lines Complete Guide →
Rabi • High-Value Spice

Cumin (જીરું / Cuminum cyminum)

⏱️ 100-115 Days 🌱 Well-Drained Sandy Loam 📏 30 cm Rows / Broadcasting

Premium export cash crop centered around Unjha Mandi. Sensitive to over-irrigation and cloudiness. Requires strict powdery mildew & blight prophylactic sprays.

GC-4, Gujarat Cumin-3 Complete Guide →
Kharif • Industrial Oilseed

Castor (દિવેલા / Ricinus communis)

⏱️ 150-210 Days 🌱 Sandy Loam to Clay Loam 📏 120-150 x 60 cm Wide Spacing

Gujarat produces >80% of India's castor output. Deep root system tolerates intermittent drought. Key care involves semilooper and capsule borer IPM.

Kharif / Summer • Millet

Pearl Millet (બાજરી / Pennisetum glaucum)

⏱️ 75-90 Days 🌱 Sandy to Light Soils 📏 45 x 15 cm Spacing

Drought-hardy, climate-smart coarse grain staple of Banaskantha and Kutch. Low water requirement, high iron and zinc content, excellent dry fodder yield.

GHB-558, GHB-744 Hybrids Complete Guide →
Kharif / Rabi • Cereal

Maize (મકાઈ / Zea mays)

⏱️ 85-105 Days 🌱 Fertile Well-Drained Loam 📏 60 x 20 cm Spacing

Key staple of the Eastern Gujarat tribal belt (Panchmahal, Dahod, Sabarkantha). Dual purpose for grain food and dairy fodder. Vigilance needed against Fall Armyworm.
