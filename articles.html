Pre-Monsoon Land Preparation for Kharif Crops in Gujarat

Preventing Drip Emitter Clogging in High-TDS Groundwater

Practical maintenance routine for borewell water with high calcium carbonate. Venturi acid treatment ratios (HCl 33%), disc filter backwashing, and chlorine shock treatments to kill biological algae.

Moisture Control & Hermetic Grain Storage Without Toxic Fumigants

Restoring Soil Organic Carbon in Semi-Arid Gujarat Soils

Why intensive urea application cannot compensate for low organic carbon (<0.5%). Integrating green manuring (dhaincha / sunnhemp) and vermicompost to rebuild soil microbial biology and moisture holding capacity.

Key takeaway: Incorporate green manure crop at 45 days during flowering before turning woody; adds 20–25 tonnes of fresh biomass per hectare.
