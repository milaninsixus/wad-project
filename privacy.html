Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026. How KrushiMitra protects user privacy, respects data confidentiality, and operates transparently.

1. Commitment to Farmer Privacy

At KrushiMitra (કૃષિમિત્ર), accessible from our website, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This document outlines the types of information we do not collect, how client-side interactions occur, and our ethical standards regarding agricultural data.

2. Information Collection and Use

KrushiMitra is a purely educational reference portal. We do not require users to register, create accounts, or provide sensitive identification (such as Aadhaar numbers, 7/12 land records, or bank details) to access our crop cultivation guides, disease diagnostic tools, or government scheme listings.

3. Form Submissions (Demonstration Architecture)

Any data entered into our contact or newsletter forms undergoes client-side validation only. In this prototype deployment, no personal data, email addresses, or telephone numbers are transmitted to or stored on an external server or shared with commercial agrochemical marketers.

4. Third-Party Links & Official Portals

Our website contains direct hyperlinks to verified Indian Government portals (e.g., pmkisan.gov.in, pmfby.gov.in, ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in) and research institutes. Once you navigate to these external government websites, their respective privacy policies apply. We do not have control over and assume no liability for the practices of third-party websites.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

KrushiMitra uses standard browser local storage exclusively to preserve user preferences (such as selected interface language). We do not deploy tracking cookies or behavioral advertising pixels.
