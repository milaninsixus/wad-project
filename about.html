About KrushiMitra (કૃષિમિત્ર)

Bridging the critical knowledge gap between agricultural research laboratories and grassroots Indian farmers with authentic, jargon-free, practical agronomy.

Our Core Mission

KrushiMitra was created to address one of the most pressing challenges in Indian agriculture: the rapid spread of misinformation, unverified chemical pesticide cocktails, and missed government subsidy opportunities among smallholder farmers.

By synthesizing complex university agronomy publications into clear, step-by-step guides—complete with precise sowing depths, critical irrigation checkpoints, label-compliant pesticide safety parameters, and official direct links to government welfare programs—KrushiMitra provides a dependable digital reference for progressive farmers and agricultural students alike.

The Pillars of Our Editorial Strategy

1. Verification Against Institutional Sources

Every agronomic recommendation is cross-referenced with publications from the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR), Anand Agricultural University (AAU), Junagadh Agricultural University (JAU), and Sardarkrushinagar Dantiwada Agricultural University (SDAU).

2. Strict Chemical Stewardship (No Fabricated Dosages)

Chemical active ingredients, formulations, target pests, and pre-harvest intervals (PHI) adhere strictly to official schedules published by the Central Insecticide Board & Registration Committee (CIBRC). We never publish speculative chemical mixtures or recommend unregistered off-label usages.

3. Direct Gateway to Official Portals (.gov.in)

We provide transparent links directly to verified government domains (such as pmkisan.gov.in, pmfby.gov.in, and ikhedut.gujarat.gov.in). We do not act as intermediaries, collect processing fees, or request sensitive identification details.
